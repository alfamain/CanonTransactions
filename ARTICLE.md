# Draft article — Canon Transactions

I evolved Continuity Keeper because a persistent agent needs more than recall: it needs to know whether a recalled fact is current, corrected, or revoked. Canon Transactions writes append-only claims and explicit correction edges, then resolves current truth by entity, scope, effective time, and supersession.
